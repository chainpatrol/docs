The **metrics digest email** is an opt-in summary of your organization's recent threat activity. When enabled, ChainPatrol emails your owners and admins at **6:00 PM Eastern** with the three top-line counts from the [Metrics](/concepts/metrics) page — new threats, takedowns filed, and takedowns completed — plus a link that opens Metrics with the same date window pre-selected.

Use this if your team wants a regular recap of the current threat landscape without having to open the dashboard, or if leadership wants a low-effort read on how ChainPatrol is performing.

<Note>
  The metrics digest email is rolled out gradually. If you do not see the setting described below, the feature is not yet enabled for your organization — contact ChainPatrol staff if you would like access.
</Note>

## Choose a cadence

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Notification Settings">
    In the [ChainPatrol dashboard](https://app.chainpatrol.io/), go to **Settings** > **Integrations** and find the **Notification Settings** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Summaries section">
    Scroll to the **Summaries** section and locate the **Activity summary** row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick Off, Daily, or Weekly">
    Choose one of:

    - **Off** — no digest email is sent. This is the default.
    - **Daily** — recipients get an email every day covering the last 24 hours.
    - **Weekly** — recipients get one email on Mondays covering the last 7 days.

    Changes save immediately — there is no separate save step.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Only users with permission to update notification settings (Owners and Admins) can see or change the cadence.

## Who receives it

Recipients are resolved at send time from your organization's current membership:

- Every **Owner** and **Admin** with an email address.
- ChainPatrol staff (`@chainpatrol.io` addresses) are excluded automatically, so internal accounts on your org don't get a copy.

There is no per-user opt-out today. If someone should stop receiving the digest, change their role or set the cadence back to **Off**.

## What the email contains

Each digest shows the same three totals from the [Metrics](/concepts/metrics) page, calculated for the digest's time window:

- **New Threats** — assets blocked as threats during the window.
- **Takedowns Filed** — takedown requests your organization initiated during the window.
- **Takedowns Completed** — takedown requests that reached completed status during the window.

The email also includes a button that deep-links back to the Metrics page with the same date range applied, so you can drill into breakdowns by brand, asset type, or threat category.

## Delivery cadence

One production cron fires at **6:00 PM Eastern** each day and decides what to send based on your cadence setting:

| Setting | When it sends | Time window covered |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Off | Never | — |
| Daily | Every day at 6:00 PM ET | Previous day 6:00 PM ET through today 5:59 PM ET |
| Weekly | Mondays at 6:00 PM ET | Previous Monday 6:00 PM ET through this Monday 5:59 PM ET |

Digests are sent per recipient, so a transient delivery failure to one inbox does not affect the others on your team.
