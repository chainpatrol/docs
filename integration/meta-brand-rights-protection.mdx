[Brand Rights Protection](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/828925381043253) is Meta's program for trademark owners. It lets a brand find and report trademark, copyright, counterfeit and impersonation content across Facebook and Instagram, and its reports are handled faster than Meta's public report forms.

Meta only accepts applications from the brand itself, so **your team has to apply**. Once you are approved, we work with you to file [takedowns](/concepts/takedowns) on Facebook and Instagram on your behalf.

Setting this up takes about 30 minutes of your time, plus 1–2 days for Meta to review your application.

## Prerequisites

Before you start, make sure you have:

- **A way to sign in to Meta Business Suite**: either a personal Facebook account, or a Meta Managed Account if your company uses SSO.
- **A business portfolio** in [Meta Business Suite](https://business.facebook.com/) that you have **Full control** of. If your company does not have one yet, you can create it when you sign in.
- **A registered trademark** for your brand, with its registration number and a scan of the registration document.
- **The authority to apply.** The applicant must be an employee or legal representative of the brand.

## Apply for Brand Rights Protection

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Brand Rights Protection">
    Go to [business.facebook.com/latest/brand_rights_protection](https://business.facebook.com/latest/brand_rights_protection/), choose your business portfolio, and click **Continue** to start the application.

    <Frame>![Apply for Brand Rights Protection](/images/meta-brand-rights-protection/apply-intro.png)</Frame>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in your account details">
    **Your information**

    - **Full name**: pre-filled from your account
    - **Email**: must be a company email address
    - **Job title**: your role, for example _Head of Marketing_

    **Rights owner contact information**

    - **Organization or client name**: your company name
    - **Mailing address**: your company's mailing address
    - **Email**: a company email address that a reported party may use to contact you
    - **Phone number**
    - **Website**

    <Frame>![Account details](/images/meta-brand-rights-protection/account-details.png)</Frame>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in your trademark information">
    - **Trademark name**: the full name of your registered trademark
    - **Trademark registration URL** (optional): a link to the trademark in the USPTO, WIPO or a similar database
    - **Registered country or jurisdiction**
    - **Trademark registration number**
    - **Trademark registration document**: scanned images of your registration documents

    <Frame>![Registered trademark](/images/meta-brand-rights-protection/registered-trademark.png)</Frame>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Click **Submit**, then **Yes** to confirm. You can't edit the application after it is submitted, so check it first.

    <Frame>![Confirm submission](/images/meta-brand-rights-protection/confirm-submit.png)</Frame>

  </Step>
</Steps>

Once it is submitted you'll see an **Awaiting review** screen:

<Frame>![Awaiting review](/images/meta-brand-rights-protection/awaiting-review.png)</Frame>

You will also get an email from Facebook confirming that your application was received:

<Frame>![Confirmation email](/images/meta-brand-rights-protection/confirmation-email.png)</Frame>

Meta usually reviews applications within **24–48 hours**. When yours is approved, you'll get an email with a link to the next steps.

## Next steps

Once Meta approves your application, **let your ChainPatrol contact know**. We'll walk you through giving ChainPatrol access so we can report impersonation on Facebook and Instagram on your behalf.
