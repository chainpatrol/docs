## Overview

Counts takedowns currently `IN_PROGRESS` for the organization, regardless of age. This
complements the
[staleness check](/external-api/healthchecks-takedowns-stale-in-progress), which only
flags items past a staleness threshold — a high overall pile-up signals
submission-format or vendor-side issues even when individual items are not yet stale.
