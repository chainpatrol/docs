---
title: Get Scan Result
openapi: "POST /scan/result"
---

## Overview

Fetch the detailed results of an asset scan by scan ID, including enrichments, rules and
checks results, and labels.

<Note>
  If the scanned asset is owned by another organization (for example, its own allowlisted
  domain or account), this endpoint returns `404 Not Found`, as if the scan did not exist.
</Note>
