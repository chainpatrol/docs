---
title: Get Median Time to Takedown by Provider
openapi: "POST /metrics/takedown-time/by-provider"
---

## Overview

Return the median time from takedown start to completion, broken down by the **provider a
takedown was filed against** (for example a specific hosting provider or domain
registrar), for a date range. Use this to see which providers respond fastest to your
organization's takedowns and which ones drag out response times.

Each completed takedown is counted once per provider role it carries. A takedown filed
against both a hosting provider and a domain registrar contributes to both provider
entries.

Same exclusion rules as the [point-in-time](/external-api/metrics-takedown-time) and
[trend](/external-api/metrics-takedown-time-trend) endpoints: takedowns that took longer
than 40 days and takedowns with a negative recorded duration are dropped from the median.
Staff-flagged filing and liveness-check delays are also excluded.

## When to use it

- Rank your providers by how quickly they act on takedowns, and surface the slowest ones
  in a report or dashboard.
- Compare hosting providers against domain registrars against TLD registrars by passing a
  subset of `roles`.
- Narrow the median to a particular block label (`Brand Impersonation`, `Employee
  Impersonation`, `Targeting Org Users`), brand type, country, or subset of brands.

## Access

This endpoint is gated per organization on the same `median-time-to-takedown-api` PostHog
flag as [`POST /metrics/takedown-time`](/external-api/metrics-takedown-time). An
organization without the flag enabled receives `403 Forbidden`. An unknown `slug` returns
`404 Not Found`. Contact ChainPatrol to have the flag enabled for your organization.

## Notes

- Dates accept either a full ISO 8601 timestamp or a date-only `YYYY-MM-DD` value. A
  date-only `endDate` covers the whole day in UTC (through 23:59:59.999Z), so
  `endDate: "2026-05-31"` includes takedowns completed at any time on May 31.
- `role` is one of `HOSTING_PROVIDER`, `DOMAIN_REGISTRAR`, or `TLD_REGISTRAR`. Omit
  `roles` from the request to get every role.
- Response rows are sorted ascending by median.
