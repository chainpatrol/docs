---
title: Get Median Time to Takedown
openapi: "POST /metrics/takedown-time"
---

## Overview

Return the median time from takedown start to completion, one row per asset type, for a
date range. This is the same number the org metrics page shows in its **Median time to
takedown** chart, exposed for programmatic reporting.

Same exclusion rules as the chart: takedowns that took longer than 40 days and takedowns
with a negative recorded duration are dropped from the median. Staff-flagged filing and
liveness-check delays are also excluded.

## When to use it

- Feed a customer-facing dashboard or executive report with the median response time your
  organization is currently seeing per platform.
- Compare a specific window (e.g. the last quarter) against another with the `startDate`
  and `endDate` filters.
- Narrow the median to a particular block label (`Brand Impersonation`, `Employee
  Impersonation`, `Targeting Org Users`), brand type, country, or subset of brands.

## Access

This endpoint is gated per organization on the `median-time-to-takedown-api` PostHog flag.
An organization without the flag enabled receives `403 Forbidden`. An unknown `slug`
returns `404 Not Found`. Contact ChainPatrol to have the flag enabled for your
organization.

## Notes

- Dates accept either a full ISO 8601 timestamp or a date-only `YYYY-MM-DD` value. A
  date-only `endDate` covers the whole day in UTC (through 23:59:59.999Z), so
  `endDate: "2026-05-31"` includes takedowns completed at any time on May 31.
- Response rows are sorted ascending by median.
