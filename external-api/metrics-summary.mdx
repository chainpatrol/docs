## Overview

Get customer-facing organization metrics and blocked threat breakdowns for a date range.

<Note>
The date range is only applied when you send **both** `startDate` and `endDate`.
With one or neither, every metric is all-time. Each metric filters on its own
timestamp: threat counts (`newThreats`, `domainThreats`, …) on when the threat was
blocked, `threatsWatchlisted` on when your organization reported the asset.
</Note>

`newThreats` counts every confirmed threat, including **pending blocks** for
organizations whose protection is not active. To list the threats behind it, use
[`/detection/list`](/external-api/detection-list) with the `blockStatus` filter or
[`/threats/list`](/external-api/threats-list) with `includePending`. To list the
assets behind `threatsWatchlisted`, use
[`/organization/watchlist`](/external-api/organization-watchlist-list).
