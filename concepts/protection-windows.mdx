## Overview

<Info>
A **protection window** is a period of heightened protection that ChainPatrol sets up around one of your launches, token drops, claims or announcements. During the window, ChainPatrol watches the asset types that matter for the event more closely and follows up on takedowns faster.
</Info>

Scammers often time impersonation and phishing campaigns to big moments for your brand. A protection window concentrates ChainPatrol's detection and takedown effort on those few days.

## How windows are set up

ChainPatrol staff create and manage protection windows for your organization. Each window has:

- **A name**, such as the launch or announcement it covers
- **A start and an end**, at most 14 days apart
- **Asset types** to watch more closely, such as domains or social media profiles

An organization can't have two windows that overlap. You can't create or edit windows yourself. Tell your ChainPatrol team about upcoming launches and announcements so they can open a window for them.

## What changes during a window

While a window is active, ChainPatrol does the following for the asset types and detection sources the window focuses on:

- **Faster detection**: [detection sources](/getting-started/how-it-works/detection-sources) chosen for the window run on ChainPatrol's most frequent schedule. ChainPatrol can also turn on extra detection that runs only for the window.
- **Sooner liveness checks**: [watchlisted](/concepts/watchlist) assets of the focused types are prioritized for re-checks, so ChainPatrol notices sooner when a threat comes back online.
- **Faster takedown follow-up**: a [takedown](/concepts/takedowns) with no activity is flagged as stale after 1 day instead of 3, and takedowns are refiled every 2 days instead of every 5.

When the window ends, everything returns to its usual schedule and detection that ran only for the window is switched off.

## Viewing your windows

Go to **Settings → Protection Windows** in your dashboard. Any member of your organization can view this page.

The page lists your current windows first, followed by **Past windows**. Each window shows its status (**Upcoming**, **Active** or **Ended**), its dates, and the asset types it watched more closely.

Once a window starts, it also shows its results for the window's dates:

- **Threats detected**
- **Takedowns filed**
- **Takedowns completed**
- **Median time to takedown** for each focused asset type

For an active window, the results run up to the current time. Select **See these dates on the Metrics page** to open [Metrics](/concepts/metrics) filtered to the window's dates.
